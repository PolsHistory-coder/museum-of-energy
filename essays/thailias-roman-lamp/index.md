# Roman Oil Lamp

For my project, I chose the Roman oil lamp because it is a simple object that shows how people used energy in their everyday lives. I wanted to learn how people made light before electricity and how having light at night changed what they could do. I also wanted to compare Roman oil lamps with the lighting we use today and see how much things have changed over time.

## How Did Roman Oil Lamps Work? 

Roman oil lamps were small lamps that held oil and used a wick to create a flame. The oil traveled through the wick, allowing it to burn and produce light. Most Roman lamps were made from clay, and some had different designs and decorations on top.

These lamps were useful because people could carry them around and use them inside their homes. They gave people a way to have light after the sun went down instead of depending only on daylight. The light was not as bright as the lights we have today, but it still helped people see in the dark.

Source: Milwaukee Public Museum, Description and History of Oil Lamps. 

## Everyday Life Before Electicity

Before electricity, people depended mostly on sunlight during the day. When it got dark, they needed other ways to see if they wanted to keep doing things around their homes.

Oil lamps helped people with everyday activities like preparing food, making things, and doing other household tasks. However, they did not produce as much light as the lights we use today. People also had to make sure they had enough oil and that the wick was working properly.

I think the Roman oil lamp is interesting because it shows how something so simple could make a difference in people's lives. Having a source of light after sunset gave people more time to do things, even if the light was limited.

Source: University of Michigan Kelsey Museum of Archaeology, Oil Lamps: Playing with Light and Darkness.

## From Oil Lamps to Modern Lighting

Lighting has changed a lot over time. Before electric lights became common, people used oil lamps and candles to light their homes. Later, kerosene lamps became popular because they provided another way to produce light without electricity.

During the 1800s, improvements in lighting started changing how people worked and spent their time. Factories, stores, and other public places could stay open later because they had better ways to light their buildings. Eventually, electric lighting became more common and made it much easier to light homes, businesses, and streets.

When comparing these different types of lighting, it is interesting to see that the changes were not just about making things brighter. Better lighting also changed how people worked, studied, and spent their free time. Today, we can turn on a light with a switch, but in the past, people had to depend on oil, candles, and other sources of light.

Source: Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Extending the Day.

## Lighting, Colonization, and Inequality

As lighting technology spread around the world, it became connected to trade, colonization, and the growth of different countries. During colonization, some places gained access to new technologies like electricity, but not everyone benefited equally. Cities and businesses often received better infrastructure while other communities were left behind (Rural Electrification in the British Empire, 2018).

Electricity also helped bring people together by making it easier to work, travel, and spend time outside after dark. However, even today, some families struggle with high power bills or unreliable electricity (World Bank, 2026).

This shows that technology can improve people's lives but can also create inequality. Governments make decisions about where money is spent and which communities receive resources. I think lighting is a good example of how energy is not just about technology, but also about who has access to it and who gets left behind (Njoh, 2016).

## Making A 3D Model 

For this project, I used a Roman oil lamp as the inspiration for my 3D model. I wanted to choose something that was simple but had an important purpose in everyday life.

Making the model also helped me understand the different parts of the lamp, including where the oil was stored, where the wick went, and where the flame burned. Even though we use very different types of lighting today, the Roman oil lamp is a good example of how people used the materials and technology they had to solve everyday problems.

## Conclusion

The Roman oil lamp is a good example of how people have used energy in their everyday lives for thousands of years. People needed light to do things after sunset, but the technology they had was very different from what we use today.

Learning about Roman oil lamps and comparing them with modern lighting helped me understand how much lighting has changed over time. It is not just about having brighter lights, but also about how people work, study, and spend their time. At the same time, not everyone has the same access to energy, even today.

Today, electricity and artificial lighting are things many of us take for granted, but even a small oil lamp made a big difference in people's lives. This project helped me see how something as simple as a lamp can connect to bigger topics like technology, society, and inequality.
